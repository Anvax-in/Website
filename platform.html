01 · Capabilities

Four surfaces. One governance layer underneath all of them.

Each surface is opinionated about citations, audit trail and tenancy. None of them lets a user paste customer PII into a model and walk away.

01Search

Hybrid search across every system, scoped to the user's role.

RAG + semantic + structured retrieval over the customer's corpus and India-stack connectors. Every result cites the source by document, row and paragraph. No "answer-from-the-air" hallucinations — if the corpus doesn't have it, the response says so.

  • IndexHybrid · BM25 + dense · per-tenant
  • DLStenant_id enforced at search layer
  • CiteDocument · row · paragraph
  • LatencyP95 < 800ms · 99.5% target
02Chat

Threads with explainability built into the response, not bolted on.

Every assistant reply carries a confidence score, the cited chunk IDs and the exact model_version. An expandable "Why?" panel renders for compliance review without leaving the thread. PII redaction runs before the prompt leaves the network.

  • ThreadsResumable · shareable · auditable
  • RedactionAadhaar · PAN · IFSC · GSTIN · UPI · mobile
  • Why panelconfidence_score · cited chunks
  • Sign-inGoogle OAuth · SSO/SCIM (Growth+)
03Workflows

Durable workflows for the operational drudge work.

Compliance monitoring, board-memo generation, audit prep, customer briefings. Humans approve, AI executes. Built on Temporal — resumable across pod restarts and across days, with full execution history retained for examiner review.

  • EngineTemporal-backed · durable
  • ApprovalsRole-gated · SLA-tracked
  • TemplatesBoard memos · audit packs · KFS reviews
  • TriggerCron · webhook · circular drop
04Agents

Governed agents that act within policy, within permissions, with full audit.

Agents inherit the user's role. Every tool call passes through SSRF and path-traversal guards. Critical-tier policies set always_human=True — the agent drafts, a human approves. Production systems, not toys.

  • RuntimePolicy-checked · permission-bound
  • ToolsSSRF + path-traversal guarded
  • HITLAlways-human on critical risk
  • TraceEvery step in inference_traces
