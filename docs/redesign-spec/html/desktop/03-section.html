The problem

Your people already use AI. You just can't see any of it.

The tools that make employees faster are the ones your policy cannot approve. So the work moves off-platform, and the risk moves with it.

No scoped access

Employees get all-or-nothing access to internal systems. IT has no way to assign the right tools to the right people.

Shadow AI

When employees connect AI tools on their own, IT has no way to track, manage, or revoke connections.

No audit trail or logs

When an employee uses AI to take action in a system, there is no record of what happened or what data was shared.

No data guardrails

AI tool calls pass through third-party systems with no policy enforcement on what data is included.
