03 · How it ships

Helm chart in, signed bundle out.

For on-prem and sovereign tiers, Anvax ships as a signed Helm chart bundle. Your platform team installs it; your security team reads the SBOM; your audit team gets the same chain the SaaS tier produces.

Helm
Distribution

Signed Helm chart per release. SBOM and image attestations published. Reproducible from public commit hashes.

vLLM
Inference

vLLM with model-pinning. Anvax does not require a specific GPU vendor — runs on NVIDIA, anywhere a recent CUDA stack does.

air-gap
Network

On-prem tier runs fully air-gapped. Updates arrive as signed bundles via secure transfer; the system does not phone home.

SBOM
Supply chain

Every release ships with an SBOM and signed container images. Your CISO can diff what changed between versions.
